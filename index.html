Position sizing

median growth per trade, g(f)your size

g(f) = p·ln(1 + f(b − c)) + (1 − p)·ln(1 − f(1 + c)) is the median growth per trade when you risk a fraction f of equity. It peaks at the growth-optimal size f* = E / ((b − c)(1 + c)) and falls back to about zero at 2× f*. Past that, the balance shrinks over time even with an edge. P(ever −50%) uses the continuous-time approximation 0.5^(2/α − 1) for α × f*, and e^(−2μD/σ²) for fixed dollars.

What the numbers assume

Every trade hits target or stop. Each trade wins b R with probability p or loses 1 R. Break-even stops, partial exits and time exits are not binary. A rule like the V1-body test (34% wins plus 31% break-even scratches) does not sit on this grid.

Trades are independent. The binomial results are exact for independent trades. Real sequences cluster by regime, so live losing streaks and drawdowns run longer than the simulated ones.

Costs move the boundary. Cost per trade shifts every breakeven up by c / (1 + b). At c = 0.07R, about the MES slippage-plus-commission load measured on the ORB, a 1:1 bracket needs 53.5%, not 50%.

Worked example: an ES opening-range breakout on this grid (after costs). V1 at 1:0.5 won 65.9% against a 66.7% breakeven. V1 at 1:1 won 53.2% against about 53.5%. V2 at 1:1 won 42.5%. All three sit on or below the boundary.

Optimal sizing assumes you know your edge. f* maximises long-run median growth only when p and b are exact. An overstated win rate pushes you past f*, where growth falls and drawdowns deepen, which is why most traders risk ¼ to ½ of it.

The simulator draws from a seeded random sequence. Each run fixes its random draws when it is created, so pausing, changing the pace or reopening the page never changes a trade already drawn. The run and its clock are saved in this browser only.

The challenge model is per trade. The trailing drawdown follows the closed-trade balance, which matches an end-of-day rule when you take one trade a day. Intraday open-equity trailing, daily loss limits and consistency rules are not modelled.

Educational tool, not financial advice. Every number is computed in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. A simulator run is saved in this browser only.
